Visual Identity: Mula Cargo (Logtech Heavy-Duty)



1. Brand Archetype & Aesthetic
Industrial Cyber-Logistics × Andean Road Ruggedness.  
Mula Cargo rejects the sanitized, delicate aesthetic of consumer delivery apps (like food delivery) in favor of heavy-duty freight endurance. It marries highway utility, asphalt grit, and industrial machinery with high-tech telemetry, fintech escrow precision, and subtle Andean textile heritage (Aguayo).

3. Typography & Technical Hierarchy

* Display & Primary Headings (League Spartan / Cabinet Grotesk / Syne):  
  * Characteristics: Ultra-bold (800/900 weight), condensed industrial proportions, tight letter-spacing (tracking-tight), uppercase authority.  
  * Role: Hero headers, section anchors, and prominent brand declarations.
* Telemetry & Numeric Data (Geist Mono / JetBrains Mono):  
  * Characteristics: Tabular numerals (font-feature-settings: "tnum"), fixed-width character alignment, uppercase micro-labels (eyebrow), high precision.  
  * Role: Freight weight (ton/kg), GPS coordinates, escrow balances, bidding prices, timer counters, and API routes.
* Body & Explanatory Copy (League Spartan / Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans):  
  * Characteristics: Crisp 400/500/600 weights, relaxed line height, 4.5:1+ WCAG contrast against dark surfaces.



4. Textures, Materials & Atmospheric Depth

* Wet Asphalt & Macro Grain: Subtle 3–5% monochromatic noise and dark radial gradients simulating illuminated highway asphalt at night or dawn.
* Reflective Micro-Prism Bands: UI dividers and button borders that mimic 3M retroreflective tape used on heavy trailers.
* Tough Webbing & Straps: Visual motifs inspired by heavy ratchet tie-down straps (orange polyester webbing) for borders, badges, and card headers.
* Corner Brackets & Mechanical Crosshairs: Fine technical corner borders (.badge-corner with gold/orange accents) giving a CAD/telematics instrument feel.
* Subtle Aguayo Texture: Geometric woven stripes subtly tinted into badges and banner backgrounds to give regional authenticity without cliché.



5. Mascot & Emblem: The Modern Heavy Mule ("A Mula")

* Silhouette: Muscular, geometric, and aerodynamic mule head in 3D isometric or chiseled vector form.
* Attitude: Tough, dependable, focused, and sure-footed. Never cartoonish or comical.
* Gear: Often styled with high-visibility orange utility harnesses, reflective straps, or sleek logistics visor/HUD accents.



6. UI/UX Component Language

* High-Contrast Dark Mode Default: Deep dark surfaces (#0A0A0C to #181C26) with neon orange and emerald focal points.
* Data-Dense Teleprompter & Calculator Cards: Clear separation between visual cues (camera/actions) and audio cues (scripts/rates).
* Tactile Interactive States: Pronounced orange glow rings (box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(255,85,0,0.2)), instant hover color shifts, and tactile click feedback.
* Technical Chips & Pill Badges: Monospace status chips (e.g. LIVE PLAYBOOK, ESCROW SECURE, BOT-OPTIMIZED) with pulsing status dots.
