# MulaCargo — Brand Identity & Design Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence & Positioning

### 1.1 The Name & Philosophy
- **Name:** MulaCargo
- **Taglines:** 
  - *PT:* "Carga Pesada, Retorno Cheio." / "O frete inteligente que nunca volta vazio."
  - *ES:* "Carga Pesada, Retorno Lleno." / "El flete inteligente que nunca vuelve vacío."
  - *EN:* "Heavy Haul, Zero Deadhead." / "Smart freight that never returns empty."
- **Core Positioning:** MulaCargo is not a taxi service. It is a next-generation freight bidding and backhaul optimization marketplace connecting cargo owners (shippers, businesses, farms, e-commerce, individuals) directly with truck, pickup, and cargo van drivers.

---

## 2. Core Pillars & Business Model

| Pillar | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bidding Marketplace** | Shippers post freight specs (cargo type, dimensions, weight, pickup/drop-off windows, photos). Verified drivers submit competitive bids. |
| **3% Flat Take Rate** | Ultra-lean 3% platform fee on completed freights (vs. traditional 15-25% broker cuts), maximizing driver take-home earnings and offering shippers lower prices. |
| **Smart Backhaul Matching ("Radar de Retorno")** | Proprietary homeward route matching. When a carrier finishes a delivery outside their home base, MulaCargo automatically pushes freight requests along their return corridor to eliminate empty miles ("rodagem morta"). |
| **Endurance & Reliability** | Built for heavy work: pickups (Strada, Hilux, Ranger), cargo vans (Master, Sprinter), medium trucks (3/4, Toco, VUC), and heavy haulers (Truck, Carreta). |

---

## 3. The Mascot & Visual Symbol: "A Mula" (The Mule)

### 3.1 Mascot Archetype
- **The Mule:** Historically the most resilient, sturdy, sure-footed load-bearer in the Americas. 
- **Modern Interpretation:** A stylized, modern, rugged, geometric/cybernetic mule head or muscular silhouette wearing high-visibility gear or sleek logistics accents.
- **Attributes:** Endurance, intelligence, heavy-load capacity, agility, unshakeable reliability. Never portrayed as cartoonish or silly; always strong, dependable, and modern.

---

## 4. Visual Identity & Color Palette

### 4.1 Primary Colors
- **High-Vis Freight Orange (`#FF5500` / `#FF6B00`):** Represents kinetic energy, highway safety, heavy-duty logistics, urgency, and action.
- **Asphalt Night (`#0F1115` / `#161922`):** Grounding dark tone representing asphalt, heavy machinery, durability, and premium tech.
- **Highway White (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp high-contrast background and legibility anchor.

### 4.2 Secondary & Functional Colors
- **Profit Emerald (`#10B981` / `#00D084`):** Used for driver earnings, backhaul savings indicators, and accepted bids.
- **Diesel Steel (`#64748B` / `#94A3B8`):** Structural borders, vehicle specs, weight badges, neutral metadata.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Time-sensitive bids, countdown timers, special cargo handling flags (fragile, perishable, hazmat).

---

## 5. Typography

- **Headings & Badges:** `Syne` / `Clash Display` / `Cabinet Grotesk` or `Montserrat Black` (Heavy, bold, industrial, confident).
- **Body & Data Tables:** `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `JetBrains Mono` (Clear readability for weight, dimensions, price bidding, and GPS telemetry).

---

## 6. Vehicle Categorization & Visual Hierarchy

1. **Leve / Utilitário (Light Cargo):** Small pickups & cargo vans (Fiat Strada, Saveiro, Fiorino, Kangoo).
2. **Médio / VUC (Medium Cargo):** Large vans & Urban Cargo Vehicles (Sprinter, Transit, Iveco Daily, Hyundai HR).
3. **Pesado / 3/4 & Toco (Heavy Hauler):** 2-axle trucks, flatbeds, box trucks (Accelo, Delivery, Cargo).
4. **Extra Pesado / Truck & Carreta (Heavy Freight):** 3+ axle trucks, articulated trailers, flatdecks for bulk transport.

---

## 7. Tone of Voice & Copy Rules

- **Direct & Respectful:** Speak the language of truckers, logistics managers, and business owners. No corporate jargon or tech fluff.
- **Numbers First:** Prioritize clear metrics: net price, cargo weight (kg/ton), cubic meters ($m^3$), pickup time, and backhaul profit boost.
- **Driver Empowerment:** Emphasize keeping fuel tanks productive and vehicles loaded on both legs of every trip.
